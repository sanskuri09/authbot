I'm Kabita Phuyal — a Registered Mental Health Nurse within the National Health Service in the United Kingdom. My work sits at the intersection of clinical precision and human connection.

Educated at West Herts College, I built my foundation in healthcare with a focus on mental health nursing — combining anatomy, physiology, and the psychology of care into a practice shaped by curiosity and compassion.

Every day I work alongside patients facing anxiety, depression, psychosis, and personality disorders — people who deserve not just clinical competence, but dignity, patience, and genuine human presence. That is what I bring.

Based in Hemel Hempstead, Hertfordshire, I am an active advocate for de-stigmatising mental health and building a more accessible, inclusive healthcare environment across the community.

As a Mental Health Nurse within the NHS, I work at the heart of one of the world's most respected healthcare systems. The role demands both clinical expertise and an unwavering commitment to human dignity — something I take seriously every shift.

Collaborating daily with consultant psychiatrists, psychologists, social workers, and occupational therapists, I help design and deliver holistic care packages that support patients along their individual recovery journeys.

My practice is rooted in the NHS values — respect, compassion, and the belief that everyone deserves access to high-quality mental health support regardless of background or circumstance.
