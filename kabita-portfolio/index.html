Hello, I'm Kabita Phuyal — a dedicated and compassionate Mental Health Nurse working within the National Health Service (NHS) in the United Kingdom. I am deeply passionate about improving the lives of those facing mental health challenges through evidence-based, person-centred care.

With a solid academic foundation from West Herts College and hands-on NHS experience, I bring both knowledge and empathy to every patient interaction. I believe in treating the whole person — mind, body, and soul.

Based in Hemel Hempstead, Hertfordshire, I am committed to promoting mental wellbeing, reducing stigma, and advocating for accessible mental healthcare across the community.
